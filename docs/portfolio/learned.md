- Voice comes from specifics. Real example texts and a few hard "never do this" rules did more than any description of a personality.
- Single test prompts hide problems. Playing out whole simulated conversations is what showed the made-up facts and leaked notes.
- A bot that dodges with a joke feels more like a person than one that explains it's a bot. Leaning on "im a bot" made it feel less like me.
- Shipping to friends who aren't developers is its own project: a one-click launch and a bundled runtime matter as much as the features.
